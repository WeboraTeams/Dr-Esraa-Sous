# Dr. Esraa Sous — Consultant Family Medicine Website

A high-performance, responsive, bilingual (Arabic & English) landing page built for **Dr. Esraa Sous** (د. إسراء صوص), Family Medicine Consultant at Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib Hospital in Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia.

Designed with a modern, high-contrast "cotton candy pink" aesthetic, interactive 14-infographic weight management gallery, scroll animations, and instant lightbox image enlargement.

## 🎨 Design & Features
* **Bilingual UI:** Dual Arabic (RTL) and English support with high contrast typography (`Tajawal` & `Poppins`).
* **Visual Theme:** Custom "cotton candy pink" palette with glassmorphic cards and smooth hover-scaling effects.
* **14-Infographic Lightbox Gallery:** Interactive medical guide covering obesity, diet, macros, and long-term weight management with full-screen zoom capability.
* **Mobile-First Responsive Layout:** Seamless experience across smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays.
* **Social Links:** One-click integration for Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
